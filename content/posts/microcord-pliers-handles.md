## 🧰 What I used

| What   | Detail                                                                      |
| ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pliers | WorkPro **mini flat-nose** pliers and WorkPro **mini water pump** pliers    |
| Cord   | Microcord, **1.18 mm**, 30 m spools (100 ft), rated 100 lb. Orange and blue |
| Tools  | Scissors, and a candle to melt the ends                                     |
| Time   | Each handle took about 5 minutes, from the first loop to the melted end     |

## 🤔 Why

Two reasons: **grip and looks.** Bare metal handles are slippery and cold. Cord gives my fingers something to hold, and now each pliers has its own color, so I can tell them apart in the bag. 

## 🪢 How I wrapped them

I think the method is called whipping: you lay a loop of cord along the handle, wrap tight turns over it, then pull the end back under the turns so it locks itself. No glue, no knot that sticks out. 

### 🟠 The flat-nose pliers (orange)

**1. Lay a loop along the handle.** The loop sits at the hook end of the handle. It's what locks the wrap at the end.

![The cord laid along the handle with the loop at the hook end, next to the orange spool](images/microcord-pliers-handles/01-orange-along-handle.jpg)

**2. Start wrapping.** Hold the loop in place and wind the long working end around the handle, over the loop.

![Winding the orange cord around the handle over the loop](images/microcord-pliers-handles/02-orange-wrapping.jpg)

**3. First wraps.** Wrap tight turns over the loop, each turn pushed against the last one.

![First tight turns of orange cord at the end of the handle](images/microcord-pliers-handles/03-orange-first-wraps.jpg)

**4. Wrap the whole handle.** Keep going up to the head of the pliers.

![The orange handle fully wrapped, with the ends still loose](images/microcord-pliers-handles/04-orange-wrapped.jpg)

**5. Trim.** Pull the end under the turns to lock it, then cut the loose ends close with scissors.

![Trimming the orange cord end with small scissors](images/microcord-pliers-handles/05-orange-trim.jpg)

**6. Melt the ends.** A few seconds over the candle melts the cut nylon, so it won't fray.

![Holding the wrapped handle over a candle to melt the cut ends](images/microcord-pliers-handles/06-orange-melt.jpg)

### 🔵 The water pump pliers (blue)

The water pump pliers got the same steps, in blue.

## 🧠 What I learned

- **Melt, don't just cut.** A cut end of nylon cord frays; a quick pass over a flame seals it. Keep the flame on the cord, not the turns.
